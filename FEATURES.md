# Product Features

## Point of Sale (POS)

- **Billing** — Create bills with table and customer assignment; split into draft or instant-complete modes
- **Draft bill management** — Save a bill mid-way, edit it later, and finalize when ready
- **Payment methods** — Record cash, card, or other payment types; correct payment method after completion
- **Printable receipts** — Generate and print a receipt for any completed sale
- **Sales history** — Browse and filter all past transactions

---

## Cashier Shift Management

- Open and close shifts with an opening cash balance
- Record mid-shift cash-outs
- Auto-suggest opening balance from the previous shift
- Per-cashier shift summaries and manager-level shift reports

---

## Inventory & Purchasing

- **Purchase orders** — Create and track purchase orders by status (pending, received)
- **Goods Received Notes (GRN)** — Record stock arrivals and deletions
- **Supplier returns** — Log items returned to a supplier
- **Stock movement ledger** — Full audit trail of every stock-in and stock-out event
- **Damage reports** — Log damaged stock with reason tracking
- **Scrap management** — Convert damaged products to scrap and manage scrap inventory
- **Opening stock balances** — Enter initial stock quantities when setting up

---

## Product & Category Management

- Full product catalog with images, pricing, and categories
- Price matrix support for tiered or variant pricing
- Category management

---

## Bar / Liquor Module

- **Open bottle tracking** — Track bottles in use, record pours by ml, and close finished bottles
- **Bottle deposit management** — Record deposits when selling bottles; process customer bottle returns or return to supplier

---

## Restaurant / Table Management

- Manage dining tables (add, edit, delete)
- Assign bills to specific tables
- Table performance reporting

---

## Hotel Module

- **Room management** — Maintain a room inventory with types and rates
- **Booking lifecycle** — Create bookings, check guests in and out, update or cancel bookings
- **Extra charges** — Add ad-hoc charges (room service, minibar, etc.) to any booking
- **Guest history** — Look up past stays for returning guests
- **Booking receipts** — Print a detailed receipt on checkout
- **Hotel reports** — Revenue summaries and occupancy analytics

---

## Gold / Jewellery Module

- Set daily gold rates per karat (9k, 14k, 18k, 22k, 24k)
- Auto-calculate gold value from weight and purity during a sale
- **Buyback** — Purchase gold from customers with full transaction history
- Metal balance and rate profit/loss reporting

---

## Customer & Supplier Management

- Full customer records with purchase history
- Supplier directory with linked purchase orders and returns

---

## Finance & Accounting

- Employee records and salary payment tracking
- Income and expense entry
- Tax settings configuration
- **Double-entry accounting** — Automatic journal entries for every sale, purchase, GRN, supplier return, damage, and salary
- Journal entry viewer, trial balance, profit & loss statement, and balance sheet

---

## Reporting

- Daily POS report and day-end store report (with lock-down capability)
- Sales summary with top products, category breakdown, payment method split, and cashier performance
- Pending bills and daily revenue trends
- KOT (Kitchen Order Ticket) log
- Stock summary report
- PDF export for all reports

---

## User & Access Management

- Role-based access: **Admin**, **Owner**, **Manager**, **Cashier**, **Store Keeper**
- Per-role feature permissions — control exactly which modules each role can access
- Extra permissions for sensitive actions (override gold rate, delete transactions)
- Secure login with token-based authentication (auto-logout on session end)

---

## Audit & Security

- Immutable audit log — every significant action (sale created, draft updated, stock changed, etc.) is recorded with timestamp and user
- Multi-branch isolation — staff see only their own branch's data; admins see all branches

---

## Settings & Configuration

- Branch/restaurant settings (name, logo, contact info)
- Opening balances for stock and accounting accounts
- Tax rate configuration
